⚠️ Immediate Life-Safety Protocols

  • 1.
    Move Perpendicular to the Riverbed:

    Do not run down the valley along roads. Immediately climb adjacent ridges to gain at least 50 meters of vertical clearance above flood waterline.

  • 2.
    Strictly Avoid Dry Ravines & Gully Confluences:

    Secondary debris avalanches and mudflows canalize through small ravines with zero warning.

  • 3.
    Audio & Visual Signaling:

    Sound 3 sharp whistle blasts every 2 minutes. Wave bright red/orange cloth toward aerial UAV/helicopter search paths.

  • 4.
    Water Disinfection:

    Turbid flood waters contain deadly bacteriological pathogens and silt. Use Halazone/Chlorine tablets or boil vigorously for 5 minutes.

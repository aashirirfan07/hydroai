Scientific Foundations & Physics Formulations

Physical laws governing the multi-source feature transformations

1. Topographic Wetness Index (TWI)

TWI = ln(a / tan β)

Quantifies topographical control on hydrological processes. a is the specific catchment drainage area per contour length, and β is the local slope gradient.

2. Gravitational Runoff Kinetic Surge

S_k = V × sin(β)

Computes kinetic surge acceleration of water down steep mountain inclines where stream velocity V is accelerated by the sine of slope angle β.

3. Hydro-Saturation Pressure Excess

E_sat = max(0, (θ - θ_crit)^1.3)

Calculates non-linear overland runoff once soil saturation θ surpasses critical bedrock capacity θ_crit = 70%.
